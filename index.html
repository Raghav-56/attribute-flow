Inherited Synthesized Arrow = prerequisite → dependent

Same-node helpers let child information reach an inherited attribute and parent or sibling information reach a synthesized attribute. In a general SDD, inherited attributes are defined at N's parent; synthesized attributes are defined at N. Helpers add evaluation dependencies, and a directed cycle prevents a topological evaluation order. This equivalence of information paths does not erase S-attributed or L-attributed restrictions.
